A Theory Of Sex-Indistinguishable Feminine Voice Production By Male Speakers

Author: Meimu Mumeino

Date Registered: July 11, 2026

DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/K53G7

Abstract

This project proposes the Meimu Mumeino Complete Modal Voice Approach, a method for producing a feminine voice entirely through phonation in M1 (modal voice), without utilizing M2 (falsetto).

The research examines whether exclusive phonation in M1 can enable male speakers to produce a completely sex-indistinguishable feminine voice, including feminine-like acoustic characteristics such as formants. The approach is intended for both speech and singing and aims to provide evidence concerning feminine voice production by male speakers.

Study type: Descriptive study; exploratory observational study; method-development research.

Research Questions

Can exclusive phonation in M1 (modal voice) enable the production of a completely sex-indistinguishable feminine voice by male speakers?
